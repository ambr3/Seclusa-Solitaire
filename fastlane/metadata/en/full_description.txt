A privacy-first, fully offline solitaire collection for Android. It requests **zero permissions**, makes **no network requests**, and runs entirely on your device. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

This collection contains the following games:

AcesUp, Calculation, Canfield, FortyEight, FreeCell, Golf, Grandfather's Clock, Gypsy, Klondike, Maze, Mod3, Napoleon's Tomb, Pyramid, Simple Simon, Spider, Spiderette, TriPeaks, Vegas and Yukon!

Customizable: 8 card themes, 10 card backgrounds and 6 background colors.

Some features: Automatic saving of game progress, high score list for each game, undo and hint options, support for landscape and tablets.
